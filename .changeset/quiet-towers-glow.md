Add `tsyn art`. It draws the index as an interactive 3D city and galaxy in
`.tsyn/art/index.html`: one self-contained page that opens from disk and
makes no network requests. `--frames <n>` adds a timeline replayed from git
history, and fallow's health readings are drawn when fallow is on the `PATH`
(`--no-fallow` skips them).
